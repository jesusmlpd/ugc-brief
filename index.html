Small business owners didn't start a business to answer email at 11 PM.

We're hiring UGC creators to tell that story. Marblism gives small businesses a team of AI employees. Your job is to make an overworked owner stop scrolling, feel seen in the first 2 seconds, and then watch Sonny, Eva or Stan take the work off their plate.

Lead with the pain

Every video opens on a problem the viewer had this week.

Don't open with Marblism. Don't open with AI. Open with the moment your viewer is living right now. The product shows up only after they've nodded along.

What you're promoting

Three AI employees. Sonny and Eva are the priority.

All three are included in every Marblism plan, starting at $24/month on the yearly option. Show the real product on screen: we'll give you an account and walkthrough.

Priority 1

Sonny

AI social media manager
  • Comes up with post ideas from your business and what's trending
  • Writes posts in your voice and creates the photos and short videos himself
  • Posts to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Threads, X and Facebook
  • Up to 3 posts a day, on autopilot or with your quick approval

Story angle: "My feed posts itself now. I just approve."

Priority 1

Eva

AI executive assistant
  • Sorts your inbox into To Respond, FYI, Marketing and filters the noise
  • Drafts replies in your writing style, right in your inbox
  • Manages your calendar and takes notes in your online meetings
  • Works with Gmail, Outlook and any IMAP email

Story angle: "I open my inbox and it's already handled." Note: Eva drafts, you hit send. Never say she sends emails for you.

Priority 2

Stan

AI sales rep, LinkedIn focus
  • Finds prospects that match your ideal customer
  • Sends LinkedIn connection requests and follow-up messages for you
  • Also runs personalized cold email with automatic follow-ups
  • Learns from replies and improves targeting over time

Story angle: "I hate cold outreach. Stan doesn't." Lead with LinkedIn: that's where our customers are.
